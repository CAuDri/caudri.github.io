## The CAuDri-Challenge
The CAuDri-Challenge (CAuDri stands for *Cognitive Autonomous Driving*) aims to provide a community-driven platform for students to develop their own autonomous model vehicles and compete in a series of driving challenges.
Each team designs and builds a 1:10 scale autonomous vehicle that is challenged to navigate complex rural, suburban and urban scenarios: adhering to traffic rules, recognizing road signs, obeying speed limits, maneuvering around obstacles, and finding its way through unknown environments.

Community is our core principle as we seek to connect inspired individuals and let them compete. This challenge is by students, for students!

### Who is it for?
The CAuDri-Challenge is open to student teams. Most of them are based at educational institutions such as universities or technical colleges in Europe. Take a look at our [teams](/teams/) to see who is competing.
There is no age limit, and we currently do not require any certificate of enrollment. You can find the official requirements in our [regulations](/regulations/).

### Some history
Many of the teams now competing at the CAuDri-Challenge previously participated in the Carolo-Cup, a similar student competition hosted by the *Fakultät für Elektrotechnik, Informationstechnik, Physik* at the *Technische Universität Braunschweig* for many years. 
Unfortunately, the Covid-19 pandemic made it impossible to host an in-person competition. After the last in-person Carolo-Cup took place in 2020, the event was held entirely online until it was discontinued in 2023. 

The Carolo-Cup was the motivation for many students to spend their little free time working on their vehicles. Without it, there was no real reason to continue. 
In 2023, at a get-together at Ulm University, we experienced cooperation and competition with like-minded students once again. That is when the idea was formed to take matters into our own hands and host our own competition.

## Who we are

We are a group of (former) students who set out to organize an annual competition for student teams to compete with their 1:10 scale autonomous vehicles against each other.

Most of us started out as team members ourselves, and we still know what it takes to get an autonomous car on the track: the night shifts before the competition, the endless hours of debugging, the fried components and the last-minute repairs. That is what we loved.
While not all of us are students anymore, we want to continue to organize the competition, because we want to give the next generations of engineers and developers the same opportunity to compete, learn from each other and enjoy the process of building and racing a 1:10 scale autonomous vehicle.

### CAuDri e.V.
*CAuDri e.V.* is a registered non-profit organization based in Germany. The organization was founded in 2024 with the goal of hosting the annual CAuDri-Challenge.
In cooperation with our teams, partners and institutions, we can provide our participants with tracks built to the regulations for testing, team areas, prizes and a platform to compete against each other.
We also want to help new teams get started by offering software and hardware resources to lower the entry barrier.

While only student teams can compete in the challenge, everyone is welcome to join CAuDri e.V. and support us.
